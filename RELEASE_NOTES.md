# Road Rash 64 Recompiled 1.1

- Added local-player controller setup and editable player names.
- Added a unified Draw Distance slider for terrain and objects, 21:9 support, and a 30–240 FPS presentation slider.
- Improved rider/bike LOD and split-screen performance; fixed distant floating weapon effects.
- Unified original and custom music volume controls and restored controller bumper navigation in settings.
- Removed forced multiplayer catch-up teleporting and the “CATCH UP!” warning.
- Fixed dependency checksum/checkout issues and RecompFrontend dependency setup; cleaned unused code and added contributor notes.

Diagnostics are off by default. Online multiplayer remains early and very untested.

Provide your own legally obtained Road Rash 64 USA v1.0 ROM. No ROM or soundtrack is included. Full tool/library credits and AI-development disclosure remain in the README.
