# Road Rash 64 Recompiled 1.4.4

## What’s new in 1.4.4

- **Smoother rival audio:** steadier engine sounds in groups, with more gradual volume changes as bikes approach and move away.
- **More varied opponents:** AI riders choose from the full bike pool for the selected level, independently of your bike choice.
- **Thrash fixes:** saved race unlocks are available at startup, with clearer route previews and corrected yellow map markers.
- **MK64 racer guidance:** improved cornering forecasts and recovery when an AI rider loses its route.
- **Cleanup:** organized source, quieter routine diagnostics and a shorter quick-start guide.

## Downloads

Choose the **Windows** ZIP or **experimental native Linux** ZIP. Optional mods are separate downloads, installed through **Settings → Mods**:

- **MoreCharacters2.0.5.nrm** — additional rider skins.
- **RoadRash64Recompiled-Optional-Texture-Mod.zip** — Crisaty’s Remastered Edition texture conversion; extract its RTZ before installing.

Supply your own supported **Road Rash 64 USA v1.0 ROM**. MK64 importing also requires your own supported **Mario Kart 64 USA ROM**. Keep your saves and settings when updating.

MK64 tracks, items and online play remain **experimental**. Online players need matching **1.4.4 / protocol 68** builds and matching imported packs.

**Draw Distance:** 100% can load the entire map, including areas used by other races. **50–60%** is a good starting point, especially in multiplayer.
