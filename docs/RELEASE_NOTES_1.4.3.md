# Road Rash 64 Recompiled 1.4.3

## What's new in 1.4.3

- **Big Game progress:** Insanity bonus-level progress and save labels now persist across restarts. The original ending plays once while completed races remain available for earning bike money.
- **Rival motorcycle audio:** stronger nearby engines, greater audible range, improved bike-specific sounds and passing pitch changes. Audio settings include a dedicated engine-sound toggle.
- **Controls and settings:** improved controller remapping, clearer Audio settings and all four player selectors in one row.
- **Crash highlights:** improved rider, bike, weapon and traffic presentation, model detail, wall contacts and the transition back to results.
- **MK64 Tag and Deathmatch:** imported courses follow the original point-based round rules.
- **More Characters:** eleven optional skins using the game's native rider models and animations, selectable in offline and local multiplayer.
- **Maintenance:** organized source modules and player documentation, plus lighter routine diagnostic and audio processing.

## Downloads

Choose the Windows ZIP or experimental native Linux AppImage ZIP. Install either optional mod separately through **Settings → Mods**:

- **MoreCharacters2.0.5.nrm** — More Characters skin pack.
- **RoadRash64Recompiled-Optional-Texture-Mod.zip** — Remastered Edition texture conversion by **Crisaty (Cristian A.T.)**.

Supply your own supported **Road Rash 64 USA v1.0 ROM**. MK64 course importing also requires your own supported **Mario Kart 64 USA ROM**. Keep your existing saves and settings when updating.

All online players should use **1.4.3 (protocol 66)**. MK64 items, imported tracks and online play remain experimental.

**Draw Distance:** 100% can load the entire map, including the areas used by all its races. Dense distant geometry is demanding, especially in multiplayer; **50–60% is the recommended starting point**.
