# Local race options and Custom Cop Mode

Choose Multiplayer in the original main menu for local play. Online opens the separate experimental Host/Join flow. Configure controller assignments and player names in Settings before entering local play.

The in-game race options let you choose AI Racers from zero to the available local race capacity (11 total racers minus the human count), pedestrian density and bike level. Match Track retains the track choice; levels 1–5, Scooters and Insanity override bike profiles for everyone, including AI. These options do not grant permanent unlocks.

Enable Custom Cop Mode to add the cop bike to selection. Choosing it limits the rider selector to the five stock cop profiles. At least one human must choose the cop bike and a cop rider to start. Cops begin ahead on the roadside where a suitable route position is available, waiting for racers to pass. They start with a baton and fists.

Default controls:

- Tap LB: cop shout.
- Hold LB for one second: toggle siren; release and repeat to toggle again.
- RB: weapon trick.
- L3: voluntary eject; R3: spoke jam action.

Siren and voice start off. Weapon Trick, Eject and Spoke Jam can be rebound in Controls. Menu bumpers still change settings tabs.

Cops aim to bust racers before they finish. A yellow Bust’ em! cue announces pursuit, and the HUD counts busts. Busting every opposing racer displays COPS WIN! before the original results. All-cop combinations do not automatically win. Both sides retain normal bike health; a genuine bust eliminates a racer, while voluntary eject alone does not. Healthy mounted human cops can roam without route-separation relocation; ordinary crash recovery remains.

Known limits: online behavior is experimental; these custom rules apply to local play. The reported missing slow body-roll appearance remains unresolved and deferred. The Windows cleanup candidate was accepted in user testing. The Linux integration is experimental and has not been gameplay-tested.
